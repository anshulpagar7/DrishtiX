Known limits

The classical baselines use a visible-band greenness index as an NDVI stand-in, because an RGB upload has no near-infrared band. Grounding and change report grid cells, not segmentation masks. Confidence from token probability is a rough signal, not a calibrated probability. Synthetic scenes are for testing and offline demo — they are never benchmark results.

Naming what a system cannot do reads as rigour. Pretending otherwise gets caught in the questions afterwards.
