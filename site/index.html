What it
learns from

DrishtiX is trained and evaluated on VRSBench, a versatile vision-language benchmark for remote sensing image understanding (NeurIPS 2024). It is built on the DOTA-v2 and DIOR imagery collections, and every caption in it was checked by a human rather than accepted from a model.

29,614
Remote sensing images, each with a human-verified detailed caption
52,472
Object references, for grounding a named thing in a scene
123,221
Open-ended question–answer pairs
3
Tasks in one dataset: captioning, visual grounding, and VQA

One benchmark covering all three tasks is what makes the router worth building — the same images can be asked to describe, to locate, or to answer, and something has to decide which is being requested. huggingface.co/datasets/xiang709/VRSBench
